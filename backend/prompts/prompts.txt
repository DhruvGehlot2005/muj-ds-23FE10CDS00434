   - "WEAK": The section exists but lacks detail, metrics, or professional clarity.
   - "MISSING": The section is completely absent and typically expected in a strong resume.

   - professionalism_score: Professional tone, absence of typos, coherent chronology, quantifiable accomplishments.
   - ats_score: ATS readability, standard section headers, clean text flow, keyword density, avoidance of unparseable elements.
